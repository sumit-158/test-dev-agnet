Review wireframe for the Databricks App of TSD §4. Two screens, each with a desktop frame (1440 px, shown at 60 %) and a tablet frame (1024 x 768, shown at 60 %), followed by the states that look different. Data is the PRD §6 golden example and the verified top candidates of plant 3300 (MRP result 2026-08-30, inventory month 2026-08). Rows and links are clickable anchors; there is no logic and no data access.

Design assumptions to confirm (also in TSD §16)

  • A-1 One plant at a time on SCR-01 (US-1 "my plant"); the API accepts several plants, the screen offers one select plus the country filter for team leads (US-4).
  • A-2 No KPI tiles and no sum of movable quantity: the same stock would be counted for several short plants (TSD R-01). The count line shows the number of candidate rows only.
  • A-3 Office use on desktop and tablet; no shop-floor or offline requirement, so no offline state and 48 px (not 52 px) tablet targets.
  • A-4 Criticality chips use the status colours only together with the word "Criticality" and the letter; "Not set" is grey.
  • A-5 Freshness warning threshold: the MRP result is "stale" when older than yesterday (BR-07 daily refresh).
  • A-6 Material codes keep SAP case; plant codes are shown as 4-character codes with the plant name underneath.
